Mission & Vision

Why InsurAI exists.

🎯

Our mission

To make insurance accessible and understandable for every young person in Europe — by removing complexity through technology and delivering honest, personalised advice at zero barrier to entry.

🔭

Our vision

A future where no young person is underinsured due to a lack of information. We envision InsurAI as the go-to financial guidance platform for the next generation — starting with insurance, expanding into broader personal finance.

The problem

Insurance is confusing — especially when you're starting out.

Most young people in the Netherlands avoid or delay getting insurance because the process is complex, jargon-heavy, and impossible to compare properly.

📄

Complex policy language

Fine print and legal terminology make it nearly impossible to understand actual coverage before something goes wrong.

🔍

No easy comparison

Dozens of providers with inconsistent formats make comparison feel like a full-time task, discouraging engagement entirely.

💸

Costly misjudgements

Wrong coverage or overpaying costs Dutch students hundreds of euros annually — a direct result of poor information access.

What happens when you choose a policy through InsurAI?

When you decide to purchase a policy via one of our recommended providers, you are redirected to that insurer's own website to complete the purchase. Payment for the insurance premium is made directly to the insurer — not through InsurAI. We receive a transparent affiliate commission from the insurer, which is never added to your premium cost. This commission rate is disclosed on every recommendation page.

Data security & storage

How we protect and store your data.

We apply multiple layers of security and follow privacy-by-design principles — data protection is built into our architecture from the ground up, not added as an afterthought.

🔒

Encryption at rest

All personal data is encrypted using AES-256, the same standard used by banks and government institutions.

🛡️

Encryption in transit

All communication between your browser and our servers uses TLS 1.3 — data cannot be intercepted during transfer.

👤

Hashed passwords

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage. Even InsurAI staff cannot read your password — only you know it.

📍

EU-based servers

All data is stored on servers in the European Union (AWS eu-west-1, Amsterdam). Your data never leaves EU jurisdiction.

🔑

Access control

Only authorised InsurAI personnel with a direct operational need can access personal data, under strict role-based policies.

🧪

Regular security audits

Quarterly penetration tests and annual third-party security audits identify and resolve vulnerabilities proactively.

Due diligence

Sustainability & ethical risk assessment.

A thorough assessment of the key risks investors should be aware of — and the concrete steps InsurAI takes to mitigate each one.

Risk

AI bias in recommendations

If training data reflects historical biases, the model may systematically under-recommend coverage to certain groups.

Mitigation: Regular bias audits, diverse training datasets, human review of edge cases, opt-out rights for all users.

Risk

Data privacy & GDPR compliance

Processing sensitive personal and financial data creates regulatory exposure. Non-compliance could result in significant fines.

Mitigation: Privacy-by-design architecture, data minimisation, end-to-end encryption, DPA registration, external GDPR audit.

Risk

Regulatory compliance (Wft / AFM)

Providing insurance advice in the Netherlands may fall under the Wet financieel toezicht (Wft), requiring an AFM licence.

Mitigation: MVP positioned as "information tool." AFM licensing pathway planned for Year 1.

Strength

Low environmental footprint

Fully digital, paperless platform. Cloud infrastructure hosted on green-energy-certified providers (AWS eu-west-1).

Commitment: Annual carbon footprint reporting from Year 2 onwards.

Strength

Positive social impact

Directly addresses financial exclusion by making insurance accessible to a demographic historically ignored by traditional insurers.

Alignment with UN SDG 1, SDG 10, and SDG 16.

Strength

Transparent business model

Affiliate commissions are disclosed upfront. No hidden fees. Users can proceed without purchasing through our platform.

Annual transparency report on commission rates published publicly.
